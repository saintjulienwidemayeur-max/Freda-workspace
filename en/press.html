Overview

In one sentence. Freda Workspace lets you register a domain name and create a professional email address on your own domain, paying with MonCash, NatCash or a bank card, in French, Haitian Creole and English.

Short version (50 words). Freda Workspace is a brand of Freda Pay LLC. It brings together Freda Mail (professional email, with one free mailbox on your own domain) and Freda Connect (domain names and DNS). The service is open to businesses worldwide, with MonCash and NatCash for customers in Haiti.

Long version. Freda Workspace helps entrepreneurs and businesses build a professional presence online. Freda Connect lets you search, register and manage domain names with a full DNS editor. Freda Mail provides email addresses on the company’s own domain, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured automatically, a mobile app and compatibility with any IMAP/SMTP client. One mailbox per account is free; the domain name is always paid. .ht is coming soon to Freda Connect (no date or price announced).
