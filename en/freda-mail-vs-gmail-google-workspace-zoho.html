Freda Mail is professional email on your own domain name, built for entrepreneurs in Haiti and elsewhere: 1 free mailbox per account, payment with MonCash or NatCash, support in three languages. Gmail, Google Workspace and Zoho Mail are good products that do more; this page explains honestly who should choose what. When to choose Freda Mail

Choose Freda Mail if what you mainly want is a professional address on your own domain name (contact@your-company.com), with no subscription to start, and if paying with MonCash or NatCash is easier for you than an international card. The domain and the email are set up in one place, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC added for you.

Your mailboxes also work with any IMAP and SMTP client, including Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, so you do not have to change how you read your email.

When to choose Gmail, Google Workspace or Zoho

Choose Gmail if you only need a free personal address and an @gmail.com address is enough.

Choose Google Workspace or Zoho if your team needs a full suite: shared documents and spreadsheets, shared calendars, video meetings, file storage and advanced admin tools. Freda Mail does not offer these.

The two approaches can be combined: you can buy your domain name from Freda Connect and use it with the email provider of your choice, or keep a collaboration tool elsewhere and take only your addresses from Freda Mail.

How to compare prices

For an address on your own domain you always need a domain name, which every provider charges for. Then compare the cost per mailbox per month on the official websites, on the day you decide. At Freda Workspace, prices are those on the pricing page, in US dollars, with conversion to gourdes at the rate shown before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does Freda Mail replace Google Workspace?

Not entirely. Freda Mail is professional email: addresses on your domain, a mobile app, IMAP and SMTP compatibility. If your team needs shared documents, shared calendars or built-in video meetings, Google Workspace or Zoho fit better.

Why not just use Gmail?

Free Gmail gives you an @gmail.com address. An address on your own domain (contact@your-company.com) builds more trust with customers and stays yours whichever provider you use. If you do not need that, Gmail is enough.

Can I read my Freda Mail email in Gmail?

Yes. Your mailboxes work with any IMAP and SMTP client, including Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, as well as the Freda Mail app.

Is Freda Mail cheaper than Google Workspace or Zoho?

We do not publish their prices because they change. What we can say: Freda Mail includes 1 free mailbox per account, and paid plans start at $2.99/month. Compare with Google’s and Zoho’s websites when you decide.

Can I pay for Freda Mail with MonCash or NatCash?

Yes. MonCash and NatCash do not allow automatic charges, so you top up a Freda Workspace balance that then pays your plan automatically. Processing fees: MonCash and NatCash 6%, card 3.5% + $0.40.
