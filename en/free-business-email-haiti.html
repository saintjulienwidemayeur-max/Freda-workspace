How to get your free business email in 4 steps

  1. 1Create your Freda Workspace account: it is free and needs no bank card.
  2. 2Choose your domain name: buy one or connect one you already own, including a .ht domain.
  3. 3Freda Workspace adds the MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, or shows exactly what to paste at your domain provider, then checks that the domain is connected.
  4. 4Create your address (for example contact@your-company.com) and read your email in the Freda Mail app or in any IMAP/SMTP client (Outlook, Apple Mail, Gmail).

Paying from Haiti or from the diaspora

Domain names and paid plans can be paid with MonCash, NatCash or a bank card. Prices are shown in US dollars; the amount in gourdes is calculated at the rate shown at checkout. Because MonCash and NatCash cannot charge you automatically, you top up a Freda balance whenever you like and it pays your plan and domain renewals automatically. If the balance is too low, we email you before the due date.

Frequently asked questions

Is the business email really free?

Yes. Without any subscription or bank card, your account includes 1 free professional mailbox (1 GB) on your own domain name. For more mailboxes or storage there are Starter (2,99 $/month, 2 mailboxes), Business (9,99 $/month, 5 mailboxes) and Enterprise (30,00 $/month, unlimited mailboxes).

Is there a free domain name?

No. Freda Workspace does not sell free domain names: prices start at 2,99 $ for the first year depending on the extension, and the exact price is shown before you pay. If you already own a domain, the mailbox is free.

Does Freda Workspace sell .ht domains?

No. It sells .com, .net, .org, .co, .store, .online, .site, .tech and .io. You can connect a .ht domain you already own and add a professional email address to it.

Can I pay with MonCash or NatCash?

Yes, or with a bank card. MonCash and NatCash do not allow automatic charges, so you top up a Freda balance that then pays your subscription automatically.

Which languages does support speak?

French and Haitian Creole. Write to support@fredaworkspace.com, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm (Haiti time).
