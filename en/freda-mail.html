Freda Mail is the professional email product of Freda Workspace, a brand of Freda Pay LLC. It gives you addresses on your own domain (contact@your-company.com), with 1 free mailbox per account, SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured automatically, a mobile app and compatibility with any IMAP and SMTP client.

What Freda Mail does

Freda Mail hosts your professional mailboxes and links them to your domain name. You create your addresses (contact@, sales@, admin@), read and send messages in the Freda Mail app or in any IMAP and SMTP client, and manage aliases and passwords from your account. The SPF, DKIM and DMARC protections, which help your messages reach the inbox, are configured for you.

Getting started with Freda Mail

  1. 1Create your Freda Workspace account (free, no bank card).
  2. 2Choose your domain name with Freda Connect, or connect one you already own.
  3. 3Create your first email address: the MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records are added or clearly shown.
  4. 4Sign in with the Freda Mail app or your usual email client.

Freda Mail and Freda Connect, together

Freda Mail works with any domain name, but it is simplest with Freda Connect: the domain and the email are set up in the same place, and the email DNS is prepared automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What is Freda Mail?

Freda Mail is the professional email product of Freda Workspace, a brand of Freda Pay LLC. It lets you have email addresses on your own domain name, with a mobile app and IMAP / SMTP compatibility.

Is Freda Mail free?

Every account includes 1 free Freda Mail mailbox (1 GB) on its own domain, with no subscription or bank card. The domain name is paid. Starter, Business and Enterprise plans add mailboxes and storage.

What is the difference between Freda Mail and Freda Workspace?

Freda Workspace is the brand. It includes Freda Mail (professional email) and Freda Connect (domain names and DNS).

How do I read my Freda Mail email?

With the Freda Mail app, or any IMAP and SMTP client such as Outlook, Apple Mail or Gmail. The settings are shown in your account.

How many Freda Mail mailboxes can I have?

Free: 1 mailbox. Starter: 2 mailboxes. Business: 5 mailboxes. Enterprise: unlimited. Aliases are free.

Can I pay for Freda Mail with MonCash or NatCash?

Yes. MonCash and NatCash do not allow automatic charges, so you top up a Freda Workspace balance that then pays your plan automatically.
