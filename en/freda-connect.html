Freda Connect: domain names and DNS from Freda Workspace

Freda Connect is the domain-name product of Freda Workspace, a brand of Freda Pay LLC. It lets you search for and register an international domain name for 1 to 10 years, manage its DNS records, connect a domain you already own and link it automatically to Freda Mail. The .ht domain name is coming soon.

What Freda Connect does

Freda Connect lets you search for a domain name, register it for 1 to 10 years, manage its DNS records (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT…) from your account, lock the domain against unwanted transfers and request the authorization (EPP) code to move it elsewhere. Freda Connect registers domains through a partner registrar accredited by ICANN.

The .ht domain name is coming soon

Freda Connect is preparing registration of .ht domain names (Haiti’s national domain, managed by NIC Haiti). The launch date and prices will be announced here; no price is set yet. If you already own a .ht you can connect it to Freda Workspace now and create professional email addresses on it. To be told when it launches, write to support@fredaworkspace.com.

Getting started with Freda Connect

  1. 1Type your business name in the domain search.
  2. 2Choose the extension and length, then pay by card, MonCash or NatCash.
  3. 3Manage your DNS records from your account; if you add professional email, MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are configured automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What is Freda Connect?

Freda Connect is the domain-name and DNS product of Freda Workspace, a brand of Freda Pay LLC: search, registration, DNS management and connection of existing domains.

Which extensions does Freda Connect offer?

Freda Connect offers .com, .net, .org, .co, .store, .online, .site, .tech and .io. .ht is coming soon, with no date or price announced yet.

How much does a domain name cost with Freda Connect?

Prices start at 2,99 $ for the first year depending on the extension. The exact price and the renewal price are shown before payment.

Can I connect a domain I already own?

Yes, including a .ht domain. Freda Connect shows the DNS records to add at your provider, then checks the connection.

What is the difference between Freda Connect and Freda Mail?

Freda Connect manages domain names and DNS; Freda Mail manages professional mailboxes. Both are Freda Workspace products.
