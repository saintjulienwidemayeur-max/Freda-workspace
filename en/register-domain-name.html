Register a domain name from anywhere in the world

Freda Connect, the domain-name part of Freda Workspace, lets you register an international domain name (.com, .net, .org, .co, .store, .online, .site, .tech, .io) for 1 to 10 years, with free WHOIS privacy and full DNS management in your account. You pay by card and other payment methods available in your country, or with MonCash and NatCash from Haiti.

How to register your domain

  1. 1Type your business or project name: Freda Connect checks availability on every extension and suggests alternatives.
  2. 2Choose the length (1 to 10 years) and keep the free WHOIS privacy turned on.
  3. 3Pay with a bank card or another payment method available in your country, or with MonCash or NatCash if you are in Haiti.
  4. 4Your domain is registered as soon as the payment is confirmed; you then manage DNS records from your account.

What every domain includes

Freda Connect registers domains through a partner registrar accredited by ICANN. Every domain includes WHOIS privacy (your personal details stay hidden), a lock against unwanted transfers, a full DNS editor (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT…) and reminders before expiry. If you add professional email, the MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records are configured automatically.

A domain and an email address in one place

Every Freda Workspace account includes 1 free professional mailbox on its own domain (the domain itself is paid). Starter, Business and Enterprise plans add mailboxes and storage, paid 1 to 12 months in advance.

Who is it for?

Entrepreneurs, small businesses, associations and agencies, in Haiti and abroad, including the diaspora who register a domain for a relative or for their own business. The site and support work in French, Haitian Creole and English.

Frequently asked questions

Which domain extensions can I register?

Freda Connect offers .com, .net, .org, .co, .store, .online, .site, .tech and .io. The exact price of each extension is shown before you pay.

Can I register a domain name from outside Haiti?

Yes. The service is not limited to Haiti: you pay by bank card or other payment methods available in your country, and you manage your domain from your account.

How much does a domain name cost?

Prices start at $2.99 (.com: $12.99) for the first year depending on the extension. The renewal price can be higher; it is shown before payment.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you can transfer it to another registrar with the authorization (EPP) code provided in your account, following ICANN rules.

Is there a free domain name?

No, Freda Workspace does not sell free domain names. What is free is one professional mailbox on your own domain.
