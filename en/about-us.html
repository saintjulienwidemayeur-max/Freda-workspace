About us: Freda Workspace, a brand of Freda Pay LLC

Freda Workspace is a brand of Freda Pay LLC. It brings together two products: Freda Connect (domain names and DNS) and Freda Mail (professional email and a mobile app). Our goal: let any business, in Haiti and around the world, get its domain name and professional email address in one place, with payment methods that fit.

Our two products

Freda Connect: register an international domain name (.com, .net, .org, .co, .store, .online, .site, .tech, .io), manage its DNS records, protect your details with free WHOIS privacy and keep control of your domain, which you can transfer at any time.

Freda Mail: create professional email addresses on your own domain (contact@your-company.com), with SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured automatically, a mobile app and compatibility with any IMAP and SMTP client. Every account includes 1 free mailbox; Starter, Business and Enterprise plans add mailboxes and storage.

Our approach

Simplicity: the domain and the email are set up together, without technical skills; DNS records are added for you or clearly explained.

Local payment: many entrepreneurs do not have an international card. Freda Workspace accepts MonCash and NatCash, as well as cards and the other methods Stripe offers. Because MonCash and NatCash cannot charge you automatically, a prepaid balance pays your subscriptions and we email you when it runs low.

Transparency: prices are shown before you pay, the domain is yours and we never sell your data.

Contact us

Write to support@fredaworkspace.com or use the help-centre form. We answer in French, Haitian Creole and English, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm (Haiti time). Freda Workspace is an online service; the terms of use, privacy policy and refund policy are published on the legal page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is behind Freda Workspace?

Freda Workspace is a brand of Freda Pay LLC, a company registered in the State of Wyoming (USA), part of Freda Group.

What does Freda Workspace include?

Two products: Freda Connect for domain names and DNS, and Freda Mail for professional email and the mobile app.

Is Freda Workspace only for Haiti?

No. The service accepts MonCash and NatCash for customers in Haiti, but international domain names can be registered from abroad and paid by card or the other methods Stripe offers.

What is the difference between Freda Workspace and Freda Pay?

Freda Pay LLC is the company. Freda Workspace is its brand for domain names and professional email. MonCash and NatCash payments are processed by Freda Pay Issuing, from the same company.

How do I contact support?

By email at support@fredaworkspace.com or through the help-centre form, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm (Haiti time), in French, Haitian Creole or English.
